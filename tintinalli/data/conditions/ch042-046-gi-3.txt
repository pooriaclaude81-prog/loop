@scenario

- Abdominal CT

@L

@S
- Aggressive crystalloid fluid resuscitation; pressors for hypotension not responsive to adequate fluid

- Laparotomy may be indicated for hemorrhage or abscess drainage

@Co
- Intensive care unit with surgical consultation (dropping hemoglobin, poor urine output, persistent hypotension, hypoxia, acidosis, hypocalcemia); gastroenterology for ERCP and sphincterotomy in gallstone pancreatitis
