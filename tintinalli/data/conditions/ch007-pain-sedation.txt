@variant la-digital-block
name: Digital block (finger or toe)
level: III
imp: Digital nerve block
cond: Urgent
act: RBR
diet: PO
ecg: none
cxr: none

@scenario
مردی ۳۵ ساله با له‌شدگی نوک انگشت که نیاز به بی‌حسی انگشت دارد.

@why
بلوک دیجیتال به بیهوشی بهتر با دوز کمتر و بدون اعوجاج بافت می‌انجامد؛ شروع کندتر.

@N
- Assess and document neurovascular status before the procedure
- Hand prone (palm down); nerves run on the lateral aspect of the finger dorsal and palmar sides

@T
- Digital block: insert the needle on the dorsal surface of the proximal phalanx, advance toward the volar surface immediately adjacent to the phalanx, aspirate, inject 1 mL of anesthetic; retract slowly while injecting another 1 mL; direct across the dorsum to the other side and inject 1 mL while withdrawing; repeat on the other side (toes the same way) {note: avoid epinephrine with suspected digital vascular injury}
- [alternative one-injection] Flexor tendon sheath block: hand supine, patient flexes the finger, inject at the distal palmar crease into the flexor tendon sheath, 2 to 3 mL after the "pop" (may not anesthetize the distal fingertip)
