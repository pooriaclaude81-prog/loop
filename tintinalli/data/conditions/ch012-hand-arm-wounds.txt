ecg: none
cxr: none

@scenario
مردی ۳۰ ساله با پارگی ۳ سانتی‌متری پشت دست از شیشه؛ حرکت انگشتان و حس طبیعی.

@why
پس از رد آسیب تاندون و عصب، شستشو و بستن؛ آنتی‌بیوتیک روتین لازم نیست.

@I
- [bony injury, retained radiopaque foreign body or joint involvement suspected] AP and lateral X-ray

@T
- Control bleeding (manual BP cuff as temporary tourniquet for proximal injuries, or Penrose drain for distal finger injuries, for a limited time); examine tendons and nerves distal to the wound
- Clean and irrigate all wounds after appropriate local anesthesia
- Dorsal: most lacerations with 5-0 nonabsorbable sutures; consider subcuticular 5-0 absorbable sutures on the dorsum of the hand
- Volar forearm and wrist: 4-0 or 5-0 nonabsorbable monofilament sutures; multiple parallel lacerations: horizontal mattress sutures crossing adjacent lacerations
- Palm (no deep injury): close with sutures to re-oppose the skin creases; interrupted horizontal mattress sutures with 5-0 monofilament; median or ulnar nerve block may help exploration
- Tetanus prophylaxis as indicated
- [complex wounds: bites, injuries more than 12 hours old, contaminated, exposed bone, significant comorbidities] Antibiotics {note: drug and dose not given in the book}; prophylactic antibiotics are not routinely needed for uncomplicated hand lacerations

@Co
- [complex or extensive injuries, skin grafting, technically demanding repair, or injuries impacting function] Hand specialist

- ring tourniquet: حلقه تنگ حول فالانکس پروگزیمال به‌دلیل تورم دیستال گیر می‌کند؛ حلقه را از همه انگشتان آسیب‌دیده بردار

- Tetanus prophylaxis as indicated
