@scenario
مردی ۳۵ ساله با فرورفتن خرده شیشه در کف دست و احساس جسم خارجی.

@why
شناسایی با اکتشاف و تصویربرداری و سپس تصمیم برای برداشتن یا باقی گذاشتن.

@I
- [foreign body suspected and not identified by inspection] Plain radiograph (underpenetrated soft tissue technique); CT, ultrasound or MRI in some circumstances (wood: ultrasound/CT/MRI better; metal and glass: plain film excellent)

@T
- Adequate lighting, anesthesia and hemostasis; inspect all recesses; extend the wound margins with a scalpel if needed; blind gentle probing with a closed hemostat (glass)
- Remove the foreign body (consider referral to a specialist for complicated or delayed removal)
- Tetanus immunization and appropriate wound care; primary closure after removal if infection risk is low, delayed primary closure for higher risk
- No proven benefit of prophylactic antibiotics for wounds that are not infected

@scenario

@why
روش برداشتن بر اساس نوع جسم و محل آن.

@T
- Superficial metallic needle: small incision over one end and remove with a hemostat; deeper: incision at the midpoint, grasp with a hemostat and push it out through the entrance wound; perpendicular needle: extend the incision and press the wound edges to expose it
- Small superficial wood splinter or spine: forceps; deeper: incision along the long axis of the foreign body; small and hard to localize: remove a small elliptical block of tissue and close primarily; subungual splinter: traction or excise a portion of nail over it
- Cactus spines in the dermis: remove individually or apply adhesive (facial gel, rubber cement, household glue) and peel when dry
- Fishhook (after anesthesia): retrograde technique; string-pull method; advance-and-cut (push the point and barb through, clip with wire cutters, thread the rest back); incision technique (nearly always successful)
