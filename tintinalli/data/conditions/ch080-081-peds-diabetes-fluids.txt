@guide

@scenario
دختر ۹ ساله با پرنوشی، استفراغ، تنفس عمیق، قند 450 و pH 7.15؛ فشار نرمال.

@why
DKA کودک: مایع محتاطانه، انسولین ثابت و اجتناب از بیکربنات.

@L
- Glucose hourly; serum electrolytes every 2 hours; ketones; blood gas {note: pH < 7.30 or bicarbonate < 15 mEq/L defines DKA}

@S
- NS bolus 10 mL/kg over 1 hour if hemodynamically stable, then NS at 1.5 times the calculated maintenance rate (avoid too rapid correction of fluid deficit, especially if calculated osmolality > 340 mOsm/L) {ci: caution in heart failure / pulmonary edema}
- [K+ 3.5 to 5.5 mEq/L and urinating] add 30 mEq K+/L (1/2 KCl and 1/2 potassium phosphate); [K+ 2.5 to 3.5] add 40 mEq K+/L; consider more if < 2.5
- Add dextrose to IV fluids when blood glucose < 200 to 250 mg/dL

@T
- Regular insulin infusion 0.1 U/kg/h after IV fluid bolus; adjust to maintain glucose decline 50 to 100 mg/dL/h; do not decrease below 0.05 U/kg/h until ketonuria has resolved {ci: no insulin boluses or high-dose insulin}
- Bicarbonate not recommended {ci: only in critically ill with pH < 7.0 and hemodynamic compromise unresponsive to fluid resuscitation}

@Co
- ICU admission; early consultation with primary care physician and pediatric endocrinologist

@scenario
کودک ۸ ساله ۸ ساعت پس از شروع درمان DKA با سردرد شدید، کاهش هوشیاری و تشنج.

@why
درمان قبل از CT؛ مانیتول یا سالین 3٪ و محدودیت مایع.

@I
- Head CT (may confirm; treatment should begin prior to obtaining CT when suspected); intracranial pressure monitoring may be indicated

@T
- Mannitol 0.25 to 1 g/kg; consider 3% hypertonic saline 10 mL/kg over 30 minutes
- Restrict additional IV fluids to the minimum required to maintain IV access
- Caution if endotracheal intubation is required; avoid eucapnea (a rise in CO2 may worsen acidosis and cerebral edema)

@Co
- ICU admission with possible ICP monitoring

@scenario
شیرخوار ۱۰ ماهه با اسهال شدید، لتارژی، پرشدن مویرگی 5 ثانیه و اندام سرد.

@why
شوک: بولوس 20 mL/kg تکراری؛ سپس بازسازی کمبود و maintenance.

@L
- Serum electrolytes (severe dehydration); bedside glucose if altered sensorium

@S
- Initial bolus of 20 mL/kg isotonic crystalloid (NS or LR) IV/IO; repeat every 10 to 20 minutes until mental status, vital signs and peripheral perfusion improve (up to 60 mL/kg or more in the first hour; monitor for volume overload or myocardial dysfunction)
- If no shock but oral rehydration not tolerated: 20 mL/kg over 1 hour and repeat as needed; then 5% dextrose in 0.9% saline at one to two times maintenance while determining disposition
- Maintenance: first 10 kg 100 mL/kg/d (4 mL/kg/h); second 10 kg 50 mL/kg/d (2 mL/kg/h); each kg > 20 kg 20 mL/kg/d (1 mL/kg/h); isotonic fluids after the first week of life (day 1: 10% dextrose with no electrolytes; days 2 to 7: 5% dextrose in 0.45% saline)

@Co
- Admit (severe dehydration; young infants with ongoing significant fluid losses; electrolyte or metabolic derangements; failed oral rehydration)

@scenario
شیرخوار ۴ ماهه با استفراغ و اسهال، تشنج و Na 118.

@why
هیپوناترمی علامت‌دار: سالین 3٪ سپس اصلاح آهسته 48 ساعته.

@L
- Serum electrolytes, calcium, magnesium; ECG for hyperkalemia (peaked T waves, prolonged PR, widening of QRS)

@S
- Hyponatremia: IV normal saline starting with 20 mL/kg bolus; with seizures 3% saline 4 mL/kg over 30 min (or 2 to 3 mL/kg until symptoms resolve or Na > 120); further correction slowly over 48 hours
- Hypernatremia: IV normal saline starting with 20 mL/kg bolus; correct slowly over 48 hours

@T
- Hyperkalemia: Insulin 0.1 unit/kg plus 25% glucose 0.5 g/kg IV (neonates: see table); Calcium gluconate 10% 1 mL/kg IV no faster than 1 mL/min; Albuterol 0.5% solution 2.5 mg via nebulization
- Hypokalemia severe: KCl 0.2 to 0.3 mEq/kg/h IV
- Hypocalcemia: Calcium gluconate 10% 1 mL/kg IV no faster than 1 mL/min; hypercalcemia: normal saline 20 mL/kg and consider Furosemide 1 to 2 mg/kg IV (maximum 40 mg)
- Hypomagnesemia (seizures, dysrhythmia): Magnesium sulfate 1 mEq/kg slowly over 4 hours; hypermagnesemia: remove exogenous source; if severe Calcium gluconate 10% 1 mL/kg IV no faster than 1 mL/min

@Co
- Admit
