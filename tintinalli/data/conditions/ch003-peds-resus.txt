@guide
# منطق
- آماده‌سازی: خط IV کارآمد، مانیتور و پالس‌اکسی‌متری، ساکشن روشن، چک لارنگوسکوپ.

- فشار روی کریکوئید لازم نیست و می‌تواند مشکل ایجاد کند (در کودک نای نرم را مسدود می‌کند).

@pearls
- ساکسینیل‌کولین ممکن است در بیماری عصبی‌عضلانی تشخیص‌داده‌نشده ایست قلبی هیپرکالمیک بدهد.

@variant peds-rsi-standard
name: Pediatric RSI
level: I
imp: Pediatric respiratory failure needing intubation
cond: Emergent
act: CBR
diet: NPO
ecg: none
cxr: portable
o2: O2 therapy: preoxygenate with 100% oxygen; thereafter if SpO2 < 94%, nasal cannula or face mask with flow by age and weight

@scenario
کودکی ۵ ساله با آسم شدید، خستگی و SpO2 82٪ با ماسک؛ کاهش هوشیاری دارد.

@why
RSI بیشترین موفقیت و کمترین عارضه را دارد؛ دوز دارو بر اساس وزن و کتامین در آسم انتخاب خوبی است.

@N
- Prepare all equipment: well-functioning IV line, cardiac monitor and oximetry, capnometry if available, check laryngoscope light, suction on and immediately available
- Select tube size by resuscitation measuring tape (or (age in years + 16) / 4); Miller straight blade usually

@T
- Preoxygenate with 100% oxygen
- Induction (choose one): Etomidate 0.3 mg/kg {note: may suppress the adrenal axis even after a single dose; requires analgesia/anxiolysis after intubation}
- [alternatively, asthma or sepsis] Ketamine 1 to 2 mg/kg | Bronchodilator, preserves respiratory drive; drug of choice for asthma and sepsis.
- [alternatively] Propofol 1 to 2 mg/kg rapid push, higher dose in infants {note: may cause hypotension}
- Paralytic (choose one): Rocuronium 1 mg/kg
- [alternatively] Succinylcholine under 10 kg 1.5 to 2.0 mg/kg; over 10 kg 1.0 to 1.5 mg/kg {note: may cause bradycardia and hyperkalemic arrest in undiagnosed neuromuscular disease}
- [symptomatic reflex bradycardia] Atropine 0.02 mg/kg (minimum 0.1 mg, maximum 1 mg)
- [after intubation, sedation] Midazolam 0.1 mg/kg or Lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg {hi}
- [after intubation, analgesia] Fentanyl 1 to 2 μg/kg {hi} or Morphine 0.1 to 0.2 mg/kg {hi}
- Confirm tube placement and secure it; cricoid pressure may not be needed (release if laryngoscopy is difficult)

# منطق

@variant peds-arrest-main
name: Pediatric cardiac arrest or hypoxic bradycardia
level: I
imp: Pediatric cardiac arrest / bradycardia
cond: Emergent
act: CBR
diet: NPO
ecg: once
cxr: portable
o2: O2 therapy: 100% oxygen with bag-valve-mask, tidal volume 8 to 10 mL/kg

@scenario
شیرخواری ۸ ماهه با بیماری تنفسی، بدون پاسخ و با نبض 40 و کبودی؛ اکسیژنه کردن اولیه انجام شده است.

@why
علت اصلی هیپوکسی است: اکسیژن و تهویه اولویت دارند و اگر کافی نبود اپی‌نفرین داده می‌شود.

@N
- Vascular access in the quickest, least invasive way: peripheral vein first; intraosseous (proximal tibia, 1 to 3 cm below the tibial tuberosity) if not obtained quickly
- Use a length-based system for weight when exact weight is unavailable

@T
- Bag-valve-mask ventilation (minimum bag volume 450 mL; tidal volume 8 to 10 mL/kg); rate 20/min infants beyond the neonatal period, 15/min young children, 10/min adolescents
- Epinephrine 0.01 mg/kg (0.1 mL/kg of 1:10,000) IV/IO, repeat every 3 to 5 minutes (maximum 1 mg IV/IO) {hi}
- [if no IV/IO] Epinephrine 0.1 mg/kg (0.1 mL/kg of 1:1000) via endotracheal tube (maximum 2.5 mg), followed by several positive-pressure ventilations {hi}
- [bradycardia not responding to epinephrine] Atropine 0.02 mg/kg IV/IO (minimum single dose 0.1 mg, maximum single dose 0.5 mg child, 1 mg adolescent), repeat in 5 min; endotracheal 0.04 to 0.06 mg/kg diluted with NS to 3 to 5 mL
- [persistent acidosis after effective ventilation, epinephrine and compressions] Sodium bicarbonate 1 mEq/kg IV/IO slowly
- [documented hypocalcemia, calcium channel blocker overdose, hypermagnesemia or hyperkalemia] Calcium chloride 10% 20 mg/kg IV/IO (maximum 2 g) slowly, or calcium gluconate 60 to 100 mg/kg (0.6 to 1 mL/kg of 10%)
- [hypoglycemia] Glucose: newborn 2 mL/kg D10W; infants and children 2 mL/kg D25W; adolescents 1 mL/kg D50W IV/IO

name: Depressed newborn after delivery

act: CBR
diet: NPO
ecg: none
cxr: portable
o2: O2 therapy: start resuscitation in room air; if HR < 60 after 90 seconds, increase to 100% oxygen until the heart rate normalizes

@scenario
نوزادی ترم که در اورژانس به دنیا آمده، آپنه است و ضربان 80 دارد.

@why
حفظ دما، تهویه با بگ‌ماسک و در صورت نیاز ماساژ و اپی‌نفرین؛ اغلب نوزادان با مدیریت راه هوایی پاسخ می‌دهند.

@N
- Dry the infant and place on a radiant warmer; [very low birth weight < 1500 g] torso and limbs in a polyethylene bag
- Pulse oximetry or ECG monitoring as an adjunct
- Suction mouth then nose with bulb syringe or mechanical suction only if obvious obstruction or positive-pressure ventilation is needed (no routine deep suctioning)
- Vascular access peripherally or by umbilical vein catheter advanced 10 to 12 cm or until free flow of blood

@T
- Positive-pressure ventilation with bag-mask if apneic or heart rate < 100: rate 40 to 60 breaths/min, initial inflation pressure 20 to 25 cm H2O with PEEP 5 cm H2O (up to 30 to 40 cm H2O only with extreme caution if chest rise is inadequate)
- [less than 35 weeks gestation] Start with 21 to 30% oxygen during positive-pressure ventilation; free-flow oxygen starts at 30%
- [no heart-rate improvement] Corrective steps (MR SOPA): mask adjustment, reposition head, suction mouth and nose, open mouth, increase pressure, alternate airway; then endotracheal intubation
- [labored breathing or desaturation persists even at 100% oxygen] Trial of CPAP
- [heart rate < 60 after intubation and ventilation for at least 30 seconds] Chest compressions: 90 compressions and 30 breaths per minute (3:1), two-thumbs-encircling-hands technique
- [heart rate < 60 despite adequate ventilation and oxygenation for 30 seconds and 60 seconds of compressions] Epinephrine 0.01 to 0.03 mg/kg of 1:10,000 (0.1 to 0.3 mL/kg) IV push, repeat every 3 to 5 minutes {hi}
- [hypovolemia suspected] Normal saline 10 to 20 mL/kg (or type O/Rh negative blood); lactated Ringer's is not recommended
- [significant metabolic acidosis guided by blood gas] Sodium bicarbonate 1 mEq/kg of a 4.2% solution (0.5 mEq/mL) IV
- Naloxone is no longer recommended; tracheal intubation and suctioning for meconium is not helpful
