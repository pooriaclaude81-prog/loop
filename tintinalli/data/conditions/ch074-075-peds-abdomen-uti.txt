@why
کشت ادرار با کاتتر؛ سفالوسپورین نسل 3 سپس خوراکی در خوش‌ظاهر؛ 7 تا 14 روز.

@L
- Urine by sterile transurethral catheterization (ultrasound-guided suprapubic aspiration is an alternative; bagged urine never appropriate): dipstick (nitrite highly specific), microscopy (> 5 WBC/HPF or bacteria) and culture regardless of negative urinalysis in young infants

@A
- Ceftriaxone, or Cefotaxime, or Cefepime (all 50 mg/kg/dose IV); ceftriaxone maintains efficacy 12 to 24 hours per dose
- After initial IV dose, well-appearing infants may complete oral antibiotics for 7 to 14 days: where resistance to cephalexin, amoxicillin or TMP-SMX has emerged, Cefixime 4 mg/kg/dose twice daily or Cefdinir 7 mg/kg/dose twice daily; if Enterococcus faecalis on prior cultures add Amoxicillin 25 mg/kg/dose twice daily or Ampicillin IV 50 mg/kg/dose
- Children older than 2 years not ill-appearing and tolerating oral fluids: oral antibiotics 7 to 14 days {note: short-course therapy is not effective in young children}

@Co
- Close follow-up with primary care; inpatient parenteral treatment for dehydrated, ill-appearing, persistent vomiting or medically complex children
